Public-domain audio fixture.

This file is the first five seconds of the inaugural address of John F. Kennedy,
delivered on 20 January 1961. The address and the contemporaneous United States
government recording are not subject to copyright (17 USC 105).

The excerpt is trimmed from samples/jfk.wav as published by whisper.cpp
(https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp, MIT). No model weights are included.
A Chinese clip is not bundled: a short recording with a license this tree can
redistribute was not available. The recognizer still accepts Chinese and
English through the multilingual model.
